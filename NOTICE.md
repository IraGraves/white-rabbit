## Textures

### Celestia Content

The following attributions apply to textures used in this application, sourced from the [Celestia Content Repository](https://github.com/CelestiaProject/CelestiaContent).

#### Moon Textures

*   **Phobos & Deimos**: Models and textures are from Ernst et al. 2023 (https://doi.org/10.1186/s40623-023-01814-7).
*   **Mimas, Enceladus, Tethys, Dione, Rhea, Iapetus**: Textures are derived from enhanced color maps by Paul Schenk, found on the NASA photojournal.
*   **Phoebe**: Textures derived from a texture by John Van Vliet (JVV).
*   **Ariel, Umbriel, Titania, Oberon**: Textures created by Ivan Rivera from JPL data.
*   **Triton**: Texture based on Voyager 2 imagery.
*   **Proteus, Larissa**: Textures based on Phil Stooke's maps.
*   **Amalthea**: Shaded relief map by Phil Stooke, colored by Wm. Robert Johnston.

#### Planetary Textures

*   **Earth**: Surface texture derived from [Earth Starts Beating](https://www.earthstartsbeating.com/2019/01/16/mappamondo/) and improved by Tom Patterson's Natural Earth II.
*   **Titan**: Surface texture is a composite of Cassini ISS and Radar maps from USGS, created by FarGetaNik and modified by Askaniy.

For full license details and additional credits, please refer to the [Celestia Content README](https://github.com/CelestiaProject/CelestiaContent/blob/master/README).
